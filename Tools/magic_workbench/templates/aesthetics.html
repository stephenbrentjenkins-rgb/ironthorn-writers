Tradition Aesthetics Guide

What does each tradition look, sound, smell like? Staging conventions a writer needs before scripting a working scene.

Coming in v0.3 — and growing thereafter

Per-tradition entries fill in as content is authored. Each tradition card eventually carries:

  • Visual signature — what a working looks like; colour, motion, residue
  • Audible signature — what it sounds like; chant, chime, silence, resonance
  • Sensory tells — smell, temperature, the small somatic things a witness notices
  • Staging by use — public ritual vs private working; how the same spell looks different by context
  • Cross-tradition recognition — what practitioners of other traditions notice when they witness this one

This doc is the canonical reference; the Practitioner Builder's "Channelling style" field cross-links into it.
